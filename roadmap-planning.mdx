## Roadmap Planning Overview

CareerEscape's roadmap planning feature helps you create personalized career development plans. Our AI analyzes your current situation and generates actionable steps across different timeframes.

<Callout kind="tip">Start with a comprehensive assessment to get the most accurate roadmap.</Callout>

## Tracking Progress

Monitor your advancement with built-in progress tracking.

<Request tabs="API,SDK" show-lines="false">
  ````bash
  curl -X GET https://api.careerescaoe.com/v1/roadmaps/{id}/progress \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN"
  ````
  ````javascript
  const progress = await roadmap.getProgress();
  console.log(`Completed: ${progress.completed}/${progress.total} milestones`);
  ````
</Request>

<Response tabs="Success" show-lines="false">
  ````json
  {
    "completed": 5,
    "total": 12,
    "nextMilestone": "Complete Python certification",
    "estimatedCompletion": "2024-08-15"
  }
  ````
</Response>

## Best Practices

- Review and update your roadmap quarterly
- Be realistic about time commitments
- Include both learning and practical experience
- Network with professionals in your target field

<Callout kind="success">Regular progress reviews increase your chances of success by 40%.</Callout>

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Overambitious goals | Break large goals into smaller milestones |
| Ignoring finances | Include cost-benefit analysis for each step |
| Lack of accountability | Share roadmap with mentor or accountability partner |
